# SkinSync

SkinSync is an offline Android application that helps users plan, organize and
monitor their skincare routines. It suggests suitable ingredients based on skin
type and concerns, builds morning (AM) and evening (PM) routines, checks
ingredient compatibility and tracks skin progress over time.

## Features
- Skin profile with skin type, concerns and allergies
- Skin-type-based ingredient suggestions and starter skincare plan
- Product management (name, category, ingredients, first-use date)
- AM/PM routine planner with step completion and reminders
- Daily skin diary and product introduction tracker
- Ingredient compatibility checker and active ingredient load meter
- Product suitability rating and gradual introduction (patch test) planner
- Weekly and monthly progress reports with charts

## Tech Stack
Java, XML, SQLite, RecyclerView, MPAndroidChart, Android Studio

## Notes
The app uses predefined rules only (no AI or machine learning) and works
offline. All suggestions are educational and not medical advice.
